# Kootenai County Chief Polling Judge (2021-2024)

## What I Did
Ran polling place operations for Kootenai County, Idaho elections from setup through certified return of materials.

- **Polling place command** - Opened and closed the polls on schedule, administered the oath to poll clerks, assigned roles (greeter, registration, poll book, issuing, receiving), and managed authorized watchers and challengers.
- **Chain of custody** - Signed for election supplies, inspected and sealed the ballot box before voting, recorded A.M. and P.M. seal numbers, and delivered sealed ballots and materials to the County Clerk's Election Office on election night.
- **Ballot reconciliation** - Reconciled ballot stubs against E-Poll Book check-ins and spoiled ballots, documented discrepancies on the Ballot Accounting Sheet, and certified the Poll Book.
- **Voter identity and access** - Enforced photo ID or Personal Identification Affidavit requirements, oversaw Election Day registration, and managed accessible voting options (ExpressVote, assisted and curbside voting).

## Framework Mapping
Election administration is applied control work under legal accountability: tamper-evident sealing and logged chain of custody (NIST 800-53 MP, PE), reconciliation and discrepancy reporting (AU), identity verification before access (IA), and role assignment across a staffed team (AC-5). Every count had to balance, and every exception had to be documented and signed.
