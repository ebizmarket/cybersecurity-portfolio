## 📂 Portfolio Highlights

### 1. The MATRIS Method™ (AI Red Teaming Framework)
* **Location:** [`/MATRIS-Method/`](./MATRIS-Method/)
* **Status:** Tested on a live system · BETA
* **Description:** A no-code framework designed to evaluate and red-team AI models and LLM chatbots against the OWASP Top 10 for LLMs. Tested against a live production chatbot for prompt injection, sensitive information disclosure, excessive agency, system prompt leakage, and misinformation. Documented [methodology](./MATRIS-Method/METHODOLOGY.md) and [NIST AI RMF crosswalk](./MATRIS-Method/NIST-AI-RMF-CROSSWALK.md). The console was also turned on itself: a [self-assessment](./MATRIS-Method/console-self-assessment-2026-10-25/) found 6 weaknesses in its own scoring and screening, and both High findings were fixed and verified with before-and-after evidence in [Round 02](./MATRIS-Method/console-retest-2026-10-27/). Every finding is tracked in a [Findings Register](./MATRIS-Method/FINDINGS.md). Listed in the [**OWASP GenAI Security Project Solutions Directory**](https://genai.owasp.org/solution/matris-method-red-team-console/) and on **SaaSHub**.
* **Value:** Empowers organizations to stress-test their AI deployments prior to production release without requiring heavy custom scripting.
