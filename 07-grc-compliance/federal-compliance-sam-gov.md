# Federal Vendor Compliance: SAM.gov Registration

## What I Did
eBiz Market, LLC is an active registered vendor in the System for Award Management (SAM.gov), the federal government's central vendor registration platform. Registration required completing the full federal vendor onboarding process: obtaining a Unique Entity ID (UEI: ZBWHT4UT7V13), assigning a primary North American Industry Classification System code (NAICS 541512 - Computer Systems Design Services), submitting representations and certifications, and maintaining active status through annual renewals.

## Framework Mapping
SAM.gov registration is the gateway to federal contracting under the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). The process exposes a vendor to the compliance obligations that follow: NIST SP 800-171 controls for any contractor handling Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), DFARS 252.204-7012 for Department of Defense work, and ongoing entity validation requirements. Active SAM.gov status demonstrates familiarity with the federal compliance lifecycle from registration through award eligibility.
