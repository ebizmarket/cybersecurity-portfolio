# Federal Contract and PCI Retail Experience

## What I Did
Delivered field technology services on federal and PCI-regulated commercial contracts:

- **U.S. Postal Service** - Device deployment through the Verizon Modification Team, supporting federal endpoint refresh operations.
- **U.S. Census Bureau** - Field operations management for the 2020 Census, supervising 15 enumeration pods across Pomona, California.
- **PCI-compliant retail rollouts** - Point-of-sale and payment system deployments at ROSS Stores, Kohl's, and Joann Stores. Work included installation, configuration, and verification of payment terminals subject to Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) controls.

## Framework Mapping
Federal contract delivery requires adherence to agency-specific security and operational standards, chain-of-custody for federal equipment, and documentation discipline. PCI DSS work maps directly to control requirements around cardholder data environments (CDE), specifically Requirements 1 (network controls), 2 (secure configurations), 8 (identity and access), and 9 (physical access). This is hands-on implementation of controls in production retail environments, not classroom exposure.
